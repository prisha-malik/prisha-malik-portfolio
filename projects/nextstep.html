03 / Service Design + Trust Systems

Fixing the Moment Before Help Arrives.

"Designing home services around the moments when users hesitate, wait and wonder what's happening."

Role
End-to-End Service & UX Designer
Focus
Customer Anxiety, Unspoken Expectations, Handover Protocols
Timeline
5 Months / Beta Service Pilot

The Waiting Dilemma

Inviting a stranger into your home to repair an appliance or plumbing line is an inherently vulnerable situation. Yet most booking platforms treat it like tracking an inanimate pizza delivery.

Interviews with 40 homeowners revealed that 78% experienced acute anxiety in the 15-minute window right before arrival: Who exactly is coming? Did they understand what's broken? Will there be unexpected charges?

"It’s not the repair that stresses people out; it’s standing near your front door feeling completely in the dark."

Designing for Reassurance and Agency

We overhauled the pre-arrival sequence into a warm, transparent, and respectful onboarding experience:

  • 01. Verified Specialist Preview: A friendly voice introduction and verified certifications before they ever ring the doorbell.
  • 02. Pre-Agreed Scope Confirmation: Photos and exact diagnosis approved upfront to prevent on-the-spot price negotiations.
  • 03. The Gentle Arrival Protocol: Automatic 5-minute pre-knock notification so users are comfortably ready.
