01 / Healthcare + Service Design

Blood, But Make It Human.

"Exploring the anxious gap between finding blood and knowing when it will actually arrive."

Role
Lead UX Researcher & Service Designer
Focus
High-Stakes UX, Cold-Chain Tracking, Clinical Friction
Timeline
4 Months / Field Immersion

The Core Tension

In emergency transfusion scenarios, the greatest source of clinical anxiety isn't blood availability — it's radio silence. During the 45-to-90 minute window when blood leaves a regional storage facility to arrive in an operating room, attending clinicians rely on sporadic phone calls, chaotic dispatch dispatchers, and guesswork.

"A doctor in the ICU told me: 'I can handle a complication, but I cannot handle not knowing if blood is stuck at a traffic signal or hasn't left the cold locker yet.'"

Turning Friction into Calm Visibility

Rather than creating an overly complex enterprise dashboard filled with confusing charts, we stripped the interface down to three essential indicators:

  • 01. Absolute Temperature Continuity: Real-time confirmation that the cold chain never spiked above 6°C.
  • 02. Physical Custody Chain: Exactly who is holding the transport cooler right now.
  • 03. Zero-Click Bedside Handover: Near-field cryptographic verification that eliminated 8 minutes of manual paperwork during code reds.
