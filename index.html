Approach

I was never meant to be just one thing.

Part science, part visual thinker, part curious observer. I like collecting interests that don't seem to belong together and finding the connections between them. Design is where I let them all grow into something useful.

(01)

The Things We Overlook

I look closer at the things people have learned to live with. Through research, observation and asking one more question than necessary, I find the gaps between how a system works and how a person actually experiences it.

(02)

Connecting Unlikely Dots

I borrow from everywhere — science, everyday rituals, visual culture, conversations and the occasional completely unrelated thought. Then I connect the dots until a familiar problem starts looking a little different.

(03)

Out of My Head, Into Your Hands

I turn thoughts into things people can actually interact with. Through prototypes and testing, I find out which ideas hold up, which need rethinking and which reveal a problem I hadn't even considered.

(04)

The Devil's in the Details. So Am I.

The spacing, the language, the transition, the moment of reassurance. I bring everything together with the belief that thoughtful design isn't about adding more. It's about removing just enough friction for the experience to feel effortless.
